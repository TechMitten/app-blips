You can do both yourself in the desktop app, in **Settings → Danger Zone**. You need to be signed in to see them.

- **Cancel your plan** to stop paying. You can keep building until the end of the period you've already paid for.
- **Delete your account** to remove your AppBlips sign-in for good. If your plan still renews, cancel it first.

Your projects are saved on your computer, not in your account. Neither of these removes them.

## Cancel your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Danger Zone">
    Click **Settings** in the header, then click **Danger Zone** in the list of tabs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel the subscription">
    Click **Cancel subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Read **Cancel your Basic plan?** and click **Yes, cancel**. To keep your plan, click **Keep my plan** instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again. The row then says **Subscription cancelled** with the date your plan ends. Until then you can keep building as normal.

Changed your mind? Subscribe again from **Settings → Plan** once your plan has ended. See [Subscribe to a plan](/features/plans#subscribe-to-a-plan).

## Delete your account

<Warning>
  Deleting your account can't be undone.
</Warning>

### Before you start

If you have a plan that still renews, the **Delete account** button is greyed out and says **Cancel your Basic plan above first, then you can delete your account.** Follow [Cancel your plan](#cancel-your-plan) first. The button works straight away after that.

You need an internet connection. Without one, the row says **Could not check your plan, so deleting is unavailable for now.**

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Danger Zone">
    Click **Settings** in the header, then click **Danger Zone** in the list of tabs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start deleting">
    Click **Delete account**. A box titled **Delete your AppBlips account?** opens and lists what happens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type DELETE">
    Type `DELETE` in the box under **Type DELETE to confirm.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete account**. To stop, click **Cancel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it's you, if asked">
    If you haven't signed in for a while, AppBlips asks you to prove it's you, for example with your password or a code sent to your email. Follow the prompt. AppBlips then finishes deleting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

AppBlips signs you out when it's done.

### What gets deleted

- **Deleted:** your AppBlips sign-in, and any Google or GitHub account connected to it.
- **Ends right away:** a cancelled plan that hadn't reached its end date yet. You won't be charged again. The rest of that billing period isn't refunded.
- **Kept:** every project saved on your computer. You can keep building with your own AI key. See [Set up your AI provider](/quickstart-desktop#set-up-your-ai-provider).

To remove your projects too, use **Delete all saved apps** further down **Danger Zone**. See [Danger Zone](/reference/settings#danger-zone).

## Need help?

Can't cancel or delete? Email [support@appblips.com](mailto:support@appblips.com) from the email address you sign in with.
