The live preview pane sits right next to your prompt. While the AI works, the preview shows a **Building...** screen with a peek at the code being written. As soon as the build finishes, your app appears and is ready to use: click buttons, fill in forms, and use it just like a real app, right there in the preview.

The preview runs in a fully sandboxed environment. That means your app is completely isolated from the rest of AppBlips and can't interfere with it.

## Desktop and Mobile Views

Use the device keys in the middle of the preview toolbar to switch between **Smartphone**, **Tablet** and **Desktop** views. Smartphone and Tablet wrap your app in a device frame so you can check how it looks on a smaller screen, and the rotate key turns the device between portrait and landscape. Desktop gives you the full-width layout.

You can also adjust the zoom from the same toolbar. Zoom out to see the whole layout at a glance, or zoom in to check fine details. Click the zoom percentage to go back to fitting the preview to the pane.

To try your app in a full browser window, click **Open in a new tab**.

## Browser bar and pages

A browser bar sits above the preview, like the top of a web browser:

- **Back**, **Forward** and **Reload** keys work inside your app, the same as in a browser.
- The address box shows which page you're on, for example `appblips.local/index.html`. Type another page's name and press Enter to go there.

When a website has more than one page, a page picker also appears in the preview toolbar. Choose a page from it to open that page. A website can have up to 12 pages.

## Live code preview

While AppBlips is building, a **generating code** card on the Building screen shows the latest lines of code as the AI writes them. It stays visible for the whole build.

- On a multi-page website, the card shows the name of the page being written.
- When [Reasoning](/reference/settings#reasoning) is on, a **thinking** strip shows what the AI is considering before it writes.

If you'd rather not see the code, turn off **Live code preview** in **Settings → Workspace**.

## Code View

Once generation is complete, you can inspect the generated HTML using the **Code** tab in the preview toolbar. It always shows each page's full source, including after small edits.

The Code tab is hidden by default. To enable it, go to **Settings → Workspace** and turn on the **Code view** toggle. Once enabled, a **Code** tab appears in the preview toolbar alongside the preview itself.

You can also turn on **Auto Follow Code** (also in **Settings → Workspace**) to have the code panel automatically scroll to follow the latest line during generation.

For an [imported site](/features/importing-sites), this tab is always shown and is called **Files**. See [Browse the project's files](/features/importing-sites#browse-the-projects-files).

## Undo and Redo

Every time AppBlips generates or updates your app, the result is saved as a new version in your history. The preview toolbar shows the version number, such as **v3**, between two curved arrows. If a change doesn't go the way you expected, click **Previous version** (the arrow on the left) to step back. **Next version** (the arrow on the right) moves you forward again.

Keyboard shortcuts do the same: Ctrl+Z to undo, and Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z to redo (⌘Z and ⌘⇧Z on Mac).

You're never locked in to the latest version — undo and redo freely at any point in your session.

## History Drawer

The history drawer lets you browse every version of your app, which is saved with the project. Click the panel icon in the header (its tooltip says **Show history drawer**) to see your past builds and refinements, grouped by chat, and click any entry to restore that version as your current working state. This is particularly handy when you want to compare two different approaches or recover a version you stepped past.

<Note>
  The preview is fully sandboxed — your app runs in an isolated environment with an opaque origin, completely separate from the AppBlips page. `localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, and cookies all work normally inside the preview, but they are isolated from the parent page and from other apps. Nothing your generated app stores can be read by AppBlips, and nothing AppBlips stores can be read by your app.
</Note>
