Everything buyers and sellers ask us first

Send a question to the concierge
How does the private viewing process work?

Submit the form below and a partner-level advisor calls you within one business hour to confirm timing, access requirements and any confidentiality agreements. Most viewings are arranged within 48 hours, and off-market tours can be accompanied by architectural plans and financial pro formas.

What are your commission rates for sellers?

Commission is agreed case by case and disclosed in writing before listing. It covers professional photography, architectural film, staging consultation, print placement, international buyer outreach and full closing coordination.

Do you represent buyers as well as sellers?

Yes. Buyer representation includes off-market sourcing, comparable-price analysis, inspection coordination, negotiation and, where useful, introductions to private banking and tax counsel.

Can international clients purchase remotely?

Absolutely. We run live guided video tours, coordinate independent inspections and structural reports, and manage signing through recognised remote-notary services. Our largest international client base is in Europe, Latin America and East Asia.

How accurate is the mortgage calculator?

It is a general estimate using a fixed tax rate and insurance allowance. Actual jumbo lending terms depend on credit profile, reserves, property type and lender appetite — your advisor can connect you with a lender who will produce a formal pre-approval.
