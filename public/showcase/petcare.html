Vaccination requirements

Keeping everyone healthy is our top priority.

Which vaccines do dogs need? +

Dogs need current Rabies, DHPP (distemper/parvo) and Bordetella vaccines. Bordetella must be given at least 48 hours before the first visit.

What about cats? +

Cats need current Rabies and FVRCP vaccines for grooming appointments.

How do I send proof? +

Email a record from your vet to hello@12pets.example or bring a copy to your first visit.

Do puppies and kittens qualify? +

Puppies and kittens must be at least 16 weeks old and have completed their initial vaccine series.

Can my pet be spayed or neutered later? +

Daycare dogs over 7 months old must be spayed or neutered to join playgroups.
