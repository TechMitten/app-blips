Our Story

A Neighborhood Shop With Big-City Standards

Ray opened the shop with two chairs, one mirror and a stubborn belief that a haircut should be an experience, not a transaction. Twenty-five years later the chairs are more numerous, but the philosophy hasn't moved an inch: take your time, listen to the client, and never let anyone walk out looking less than their best.

Every barber on our team is licensed, continuously trained, and obsessed with the details — from the crispness of a lineup to the temperature of a hot towel.
