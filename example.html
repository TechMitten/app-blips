Our Story

Seven years, one long walk

From a rainy bookstore in Portland to a vineyard in Napa, here are the small moments that brought us here.

October 2018

A rainy afternoon, one shared umbrella

We reached for the same copy of Pride and Prejudice in a tiny Portland bookshop. Adam offered his umbrella, Jenny offered coffee, and we talked until the shop lights flickered off.

Summer 2021

A little apartment with a big kitchen

We moved in together above a bakery in Oakland. Jenny painted the walls sage green; Adam planted far too many herbs on the fire escape. Sunday pasta nights became sacred.

May 2024

A ring hidden in a picnic basket

On a hillside above the vineyards, Adam produced a ring wrapped in a napkin next to the cheese. Jenny said yes before he finished the sentence — twice, just to be sure.

June 2026

Now — the part with all of you

We're gathering our favourite people under the olive trees at Willowbrook Estate. Come hungry, come dancing, and stay late. We can't wait to celebrate with you.

Good to Know

Questions, answered

Are children invited?

We love your little ones, but we've chosen an adults-only celebration with the exception of immediate family. A children's corner with a sitter is available during the ceremony if you need it — just note it in your RSVP.

Is there parking at the estate?

Yes — complimentary valet parking opens at 3:15 PM at the main gate on Silverado Trail. We also run a shuttle from downtown Napa at 3:00 PM and back at 11:30 PM.

What should I wear?

Garden formal: midi or long dresses, suits or jackets, and soft colours that pair well with olive groves. The ceremony is on grass and gravel, so block heels or flats are your friend.

Can I bring a plus one?

Your invitation will name everyone we've saved a seat for. If you're unsure whether your partner is included, email us at hello@jennywedding.com and we'll sort it out.
